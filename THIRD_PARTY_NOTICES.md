# Third-party data notices

## CC-CEDICT

The database enrichment workflow can import data from CC-CEDICT, a community-maintained Chinese-English dictionary published by MDBG. CC-CEDICT provides pinyin, English definitions, and traditional/simplified headwords.

- Source and download: [CC-CEDICT download page](https://cc-cedict.org/editor/editor.php?handler=Download)
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- Attribution: CC-CEDICT is published by MDBG and maintained by the CC-CEDICT editors and contributors; it builds on CEDICT, copyright 1997–1998 Paul Andrew Denisowski.

The local database records `CC-CEDICT` and `CC BY-SA 4.0` on imported readings and definitions. If you redistribute CC-CEDICT-derived material, retain attribution, link the license, identify changes, and follow the license's ShareAlike requirement.

## Other candidate sources

- **Unicode Unihan:** The Unicode Consortium's Unihan database supplies character readings, English definitions, and radical/stroke properties. Unicode data files are generally under the [Unicode License v3](https://www.unicode.org/policies/licensing_policy.html). Source: [Unihan lookup and download information](https://www.unicode.org/charts/unihan.html).
- **Make Me a Hanzi dictionary data:** Provides learner-oriented character definitions, pinyin, and decompositions. Its `dictionary.txt` data is under [LGPL-3.0-or-later](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi/blob/master/COPYING); graphics data has separate terms and is not part of the dictionary fallback reviewed here.
- **Tatoeba example sentences:** Sentence text downloads are under CC BY 2.0 FR. Retain sentence attribution and source links as described in [Tatoeba's reuse guidance](https://en.wiki.tatoeba.org/articles/show/faq). Audio has separate contributor-selected licenses and needs separate review.
